Overview

What is HANALYST SmartFix?

A tool developed and used exclusively by the Inetum SAP team — specialists in ECC to S/4HANA migrations for over 10 years and with more than 100 completed migrations. SmartFix is the accelerator that enables our team to deliver faster, safer, higher-quality migrations in native ABAP, directly within the client's SAP system.

🔍

Intelligent Scanner

Analyses packages, programs, classes and function groups. Integrates with ATC (ABAP Test Cockpit) for automatic findings.

🧠

Multi-LLM with Judge

Multiple AI models (Claude, Mistral, GPT and others) generate proposals in parallel. A "judge" layer automatically selects the best response.

📚

Inetum Knowledge Base

Integrates proprietary cookbooks, SAP notes and guidelines developed over 10+ years of migration experience — feeding the remediation engine with real project context. The accumulated expertise of the Inetum team encoded as living rules.

⚡

Deterministic Rules

For predictable transformations, applies exact rules without AI — faster, cheaper, guaranteed results.

✅

Individual Approval

Side-by-side comparison screen (before/after). Each change can be approved or rejected individually before being applied.

Process

How It Works

6 simple steps from scan to production deployment

1

Select

Choose the package or ABAP objects to analyse (PROG, CLAS, FUGR, INTF)

2

Analyse

SmartFix scans the code and identifies legacy patterns — with or without ATC

3

Remediate

Multiple LLMs generate proposals in parallel via SAP AI Core, enriched by the Inetum Knowledge Base. The judge picks the best.

4

Review

Side-by-side screen inside S/4HANA — approve or reject each change individually

5

Apply

One click applies all approved changes with a full audit trail

6

Deploy

Create transport requests and bring S/4HANA-ready code to production
